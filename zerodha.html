Import Trade Book
Zerodha's tradebook export is downloaded one financial year at a time. Equity and Mutual Fund trade books are kept fully separate, so you'll be asked which one you're uploading first — then pick the year and it's saved. Matched trades go to Current Trades below; trades for a holding you don't currently track are kept under Closed Trades instead of being dropped.
Name Matching
If a holding's name doesn't line up with what Zerodha calls it in this CSV (e.g. holding "PGINVIT" but the tradebook symbol is "PGINVIT-IV"), search for the holding and save the CSV name here once — future uploads will match it automatically. Shared with the Dividends tab.
